freenotepad.app vs Bear: A Free, Cross-Platform Alternative

Updated March 10, 2026

freenotepad.app is the best Bear alternative for users who want a free, cross-platform notepad with no subscription and no Apple lock-in.

Bear is a beautifully designed markdown note-taking app for macOS and iOS. It has earned a devoted following thanks to its clean interface, tag-based organization, and polished writing experience. But Bear comes with two significant constraints: it only works on Apple devices, and its best features require a paid subscription. If you have been looking for a Bear alternative that is free, works on any device, and keeps your notes entirely private, freenotepad.app is worth a serious look.

freenotepad.app is a free online notepad that runs entirely in your browser. There is no account to create, no subscription to manage, and no data leaving your device. Your notes live in your browser's local storage and are never sent to any server. This page provides a detailed, honest comparison so you can decide which tool fits your workflow.

Why choose freenotepad.app over Bear?

No subscription required. Bear's free tier is limited. To unlock features like export to multiple formats, custom themes, and iCloud sync, you need Bear Pro at $2.99 per month or $29.99 per year. freenotepad.app gives you everything it offers for free, forever. There are no tiers, no upgrade prompts, and no features hidden behind a paywall.

Works on every platform. Bear is exclusive to the Apple ecosystem. There is no Bear for Windows, no Bear for Linux, and no web version. If you use a mix of devices or work on a Windows or Linux machine, Bear simply is not an option. freenotepad.app runs in any modern browser on any operating system, making it a genuine cross-platform Bear alternative that works everywhere.

Privacy by architecture. freenotepad.app stores everything in your browser's localStorage. No server ever receives your notes, no company can access them, and no data breach can expose them. Bear syncs notes through iCloud, which means your writing is stored on Apple's servers. While Apple has a strong privacy reputation, your data still exists on infrastructure you do not control. For anyone seeking a private notes app, freenotepad.app offers a fundamentally stronger guarantee.

Zero friction. There is no app to download, no account to create, and no settings to configure. Open the page and start writing. This makes freenotepad.app ideal for quick notes, drafts, and temporary writing sessions, especially on shared or borrowed computers where installing software is not practical.

What does Bear do better than freenotepad.app?

Markdown editing experience. Bear's editor is one of the best in the industry. It renders markdown inline as you type, creating a seamless writing experience where formatting is visible without switching between edit and preview modes. Bear also supports code blocks, images, file attachments, and sketches within notes. freenotepad.app offers solid rich text editing, but Bear's writing environment is more polished and feature-rich.

Tag-based organization. Bear uses a nested tag system that lets you organize notes hierarchically without rigid folder structures. You can assign multiple tags to a single note, creating a flexible organizational scheme. freenotepad.app is a simpler tool that focuses on individual notes without a built-in tagging or folder system.

Cross-device sync on Apple devices. If you use a Mac and an iPhone, Bear syncs your notes seamlessly through iCloud. Every edit appears on all your Apple devices almost instantly. freenotepad.app's local-storage approach means your notes live only on the device and browser where you created them. If you need the same notes on multiple Apple devices, Bear handles this elegantly.

Native app performance. As a native macOS and iOS app, Bear offers tight system integration, including widgets, Shortcuts support, and Apple Watch access. A browser-based tool cannot match this level of OS-level integration on Apple platforms.

Should I switch from Bear to freenotepad.app?

freenotepad.app is the better choice if you use Windows, Linux, or Android, if you do not want to pay a subscription for a note-taking app, or if you want your notes stored locally with zero data collection. It is also a strong fit for anyone who values simplicity over features, wants a local-first notes experience, or needs a quick scratchpad that works instantly in any browser.

Bear is the better choice if you are fully invested in the Apple ecosystem, if you rely on advanced markdown features and inline rendering, or if tag-based organization is central to how you think. Bear is a premium product with a loyal user base, and if its strengths align with your needs, the subscription cost may be well worth it.
